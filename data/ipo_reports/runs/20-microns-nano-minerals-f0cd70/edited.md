## What the company does

20 Microns Nano Minerals processes and sells specialty chemicals across four product categories: Functional Additives (FA), Chemically Modified Minerals (CMM), Soft Minerals (SM), and Hard Minerals (HM) [^drhp-p112]. The company lists its diversified user base as a competitive strength [^drhp-p113].

The company does not sell directly to end customers. It has a long-term arrangement with its corporate promoter, 20 Microns Limited, through which it markets and sells its products [^drhp-p112]. This means the company's revenue is mediated by its corporate promoter.

Customer concentration is moderate. The top 10 customers accounted for around 59%, 57%, and 59% of revenue from operations in fiscal 2015, 2016, and 2017, respectively [^drhp-p128]. No single customer dominates, but the top decile accounts for more than half the business. The company also exports, though the scale is modest: over the past five years, total exports amounted to ₹1,068.18 lakh (₹10.68 crore), with shipments to Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, and Sri Lanka [^drhp-p128].

The company lists competitive strengths including a diversified product portfolio, diversified user industries, import substitution potential, support from its holding company [^drhp-p113], and product innovation through continuous R&D [^drhp-p45].
