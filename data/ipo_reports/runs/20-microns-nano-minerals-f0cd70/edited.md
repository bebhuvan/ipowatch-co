# 20 Microns Nano Minerals: A Specialty Chemicals IPO with 99.8% Promoter Control

20 Microns Nano Minerals Limited processes and sells specialty chemicals — the kind of industrial additives that go into paints, plastics, cosmetics, and construction materials. The company is tiny by public-market standards, with total assets of about ₹30 crore as of September 2017[^drhp-p175], and it is almost entirely owned by its promoters, who hold 99.76% of the pre-issue equity.[^drhp-p155] The IPO is a fresh issue of shares: the company, not existing shareholders, will sell new equity to the public. Proceeds are earmarked for a new manufacturing facility in Gujarat and for general corporate purposes.[^drhp-p75] The shares will list on the SME platform of the National Stock Exchange, called NSE EMERGE.[^drhp-p75] The offer size and price band have not yet been disclosed in the draft red herring prospectus (DRHP).

## What the company does

The company buys raw minerals, processes them into specialty chemicals, and sells them to industrial customers. Its product range falls into four categories: Functional Additives (FA), Chemically Modified Minerals (CMM), Soft Minerals (SM), and Hard Minerals (HM).[^drhp-p112] These are inputs for industries ranging from paints and coatings to plastics, rubber, adhesives, and personal care.

The company depends on a long-term arrangement with its corporate promoter, 20 Microns Limited, to market and sell its products through the promoter's network.[^drhp-p112] This is not an arm's-length relationship with an independent distributor; it is a dependency on the parent company that also controls the board. The filing lists this support from the holding company as a competitive strength, alongside a diversified product portfolio, product innovation through R&D, and a strong management team.[^drhp-p45][^drhp-p113]

In each of the three financial years FY2015, FY2016, and FY2017, the top ten customers accounted for roughly 59%, 57%, and 59% of revenue from operations, respectively.[^drhp-p128] That means the company's revenue depends heavily on a small group of buyers. The filing does not name these customers or disclose whether any single customer accounts for more than 10% of revenue.

On the export side, the company has shipped products to Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, and Sri Lanka over the past five years, with total export revenue of ₹1,068.18 lakh (about ₹10.68 crore).[^drhp-p128] The filing does not break this down by year or by country.

## Industry and market

The Indian chemicals industry (excluding pharmaceuticals and fertilisers) was estimated at ₹5,800–6,200 billion in FY2017, according to CRISIL Research.[^drhp-p92] Within that, the specialty chemicals segment — the space 20 Microns Nano Minerals occupies — accounted for roughly ₹1,000 billion, or about 17% of the total.[^drhp-p92][^drhp-p94]

The specialty chemicals industry grew at a 10–12% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) from FY2014 to FY2017, and CRISIL forecasts 12–14% CAGR.[^drhp-p94] The industry is highly fragmented: small and medium enterprises hold 70–75% of the market share.[^drhp-p94] Only a few Indian players have the scale or capability to compete with global giants on product development and innovation.[^drhp-p95]

The DRHP cites several demand drivers: rising urbanisation (the urban population was 31% in 2011)[^drhp-p90], a growing middle class that could reach 148 million households by 2030[^drhp-p95], and increased consumption intensity[^drhp-p95]. On the regulatory side, the government has released a Draft National Chemical Policy aimed at increasing the chemicals sector's share of GDP, has approved 100% foreign direct investment in the sector, and has established four Petroleum, Chemicals and Petrochemicals Investment Regions (PCPIRs) with proposed investments of about ₹9 trillion.[^drhp-p95][^drhp-p96][^drhp-p111] The Goods and Services Tax (GST) came into effect in July 2017, replacing multiple indirect taxes with a single tax.[^drhp-p111] For exports to Europe, companies must comply with the REACH regulation (Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals).[^drhp-p110]
