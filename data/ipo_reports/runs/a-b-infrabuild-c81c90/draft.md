# A B Infrabuild Limited: inside the DRHP

A B Infrabuild Limited builds the physical infrastructure that Mumbai and its railway network depend on — platforms, station buildings, bridges, roads — and operates a ready-mix concrete plant to supply its own projects. The company is proposing a fresh issue of 44,28,000 equity shares, with the proceeds earmarked primarily to fund working capital. [^drhp-p29] [^drhp-p48]

The company counts Western Railway, Central Railway and the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai among its major clients; it has been working with Western Railway since 2006. [^drhp-p63] [^drhp-p67] Its order book is concentrated, with one client — Western Railway — alone accounting for 52% of the orders on hand. [^drhp-p65] The filing makes clear that this concentration is both a strength and a vulnerability.

## What the company does

A B Infrabuild operates in two segments: creation of infrastructure and operating a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant. [^drhp-p63] On the infrastructure side, it builds railways (designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion and laying of railway tracks), bridges (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed and truss bridges), and roads (bituminous and concrete). [^drhp-p71] Its RMC plant at Kashimira, Thane, is described as a state-of-the-art facility. [^drhp-p67]

Contracts are won through a competitive tender process. The client floats a tender, a pre-bid meeting is held to resolve queries, contractors bid, and the job is awarded to the lowest quote. The winning contractor must then deposit a performance guarantee before site handover and execution. [^drhp-p72] This is a standard government procurement model — low bid wins, and the contractor bears the upfront cost of the guarantee.

The company's major customers are overwhelmingly government and public-sector entities: Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd (MRVCL), Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA), and Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, alongside a few private firms such as Sky Way Infrastructure Private Limited, Dev Engineers, GCC Hotel Private Ltd and Krypton Corp. [^drhp-p63] For the year ended March 31, 2018, the top customers accounted for 57% of total revenue. [^drhp-p67]

The order book is heavily weighted toward Western Railway, which contributed 52% of the orders on hand. [^drhp-p65] The company uses a "box pushing" technique for executing projects, and claims a healthy order book, experienced management with over 20 years in the infrastructure industry, and investment in advanced machinery. [^drhp-p67]

The company's own SWOT analysis identifies three key dependencies that could hurt it: the slow pace of government projects, a working capital crunch that may affect profitability, and changes in government policies. [^drhp-p76] These are not hypothetical — they are structural features of the infrastructure contracting business in India.
