# A B Infrabuild Limited: inside the DRHP

A B Infrabuild is an infrastructure contractor that builds railway platforms, bridges, and roads for government clients, and also manufactures ready-mix concrete at its Thane facility. The company is bringing an SME IPO of 44,28,000 equity shares of ₹10 each at a price yet to be determined, primarily to raise working capital. [^drhp-p29] [^drhp-p48]

## What the company does

The company operates in two segments: infrastructure creation and ready-mix concrete (RMC) manufacturing. [^drhp-p63] On the infrastructure side, it executes contracts for railway platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, track laying, bridge works (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss bridges), and bituminous and concrete roads. [^drhp-p71] Its RMC plant at Kashimira, Thane District, Maharashtra, produces ready-mix concrete using what the company describes as state-of-the-art machinery and quality control systems. [^drhp-p67]

Contracts are won through a competitive bidding process. The client floats a tender, holds a pre-bid meeting, and awards the job to the lowest bidder. The winning contractor must deposit a performance guarantee before receiving the drawings and site handover. [^drhp-p72] The company uses the box-pushing technique for bridge works, a method that allows construction without disrupting traffic above. [^drhp-p67]

**Customer concentration is the defining feature of this business.** The company's major clients include Western Railway, Central Railway, Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd (MRVCL), Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA), Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, and a handful of private firms. [^drhp-p63] Western Railway alone contributed 52% of the orders on hand. [^drhp-p65] The top customers collectively accounted for 57% of total revenue in fiscal 2018. [^drhp-p67] The company has worked with Western Railway since 2006. [^drhp-p67]

The company operates primarily in Mumbai and is gradually expanding within Maharashtra. It describes itself as a major player in developing Mumbai's infrastructure. [^drhp-p67]

## Industry and market

The construction sector in India is highly fragmented and working capital intensive, contributing roughly 8% to the country's GDP. [^drhp-p58] The filing cites an investment requirement of ₹50 trillion (US$777.73 billion) in infrastructure by 2022 for sustainable development. [^drhp-p22]

Growth drivers named in the DRHP include government flagship programs — 100 Smart Cities Mission, Atal Mission for Urban Rejuvenation and Transformation (AMRUT), Make in India, and Power for All. [^drhp-p58] The filing also mentions eased foreign direct investment norms in 15 sectors including real estate and construction, rising urbanization and disposable income, [^drhp-p59] and India's working-age population projected to reach 64% by 2021. [^drhp-p62] The logistics sector was expected to grow at a CAGR of 10.5% from US$160 billion in 2017 to US$215 billion by 2020. [^drhp-p59]

The filing also acknowledges headwinds: the slow pace of government projects, a working capital crunch that could affect profitability, and changes in government policies. Competition comes from both organized and unorganized players. [^drhp-p76]

## The offer and its objectives

The issue comprises 44,28,000 equity shares of ₹10 each. [^drhp-p29] The issue price has not yet been set — it will be determined by the company in consultation with the lead manager, Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited. The registrar is Bigshare Services Private Limited. [^drhp-p31]

The primary use of proceeds is to meet working capital requirements, for which the company has allocated ₹1,130 lakhs. The remainder will go toward general corporate purposes, the amount of which is not disclosed. [^drhp-p48]

Two governance points worth noting: the company has not appointed any independent agency to appraise the proposed project, nor has it appointed a monitoring agency for fund deployment. Fund deployment will be monitored by the Audit Committee. [^drhp-p18]

## Material risks

[^drhp-p12] [^drhp-p67] The company has worked with Western Railway since 2006. **Negative cash flows and working capital risk.** The company reported negative cash flows in recent financial years.
