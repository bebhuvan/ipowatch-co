## What the company does

A contractor does not invent work; it responds to tenders. The DRHP describes the cycle plainly: a client floats a tender for a specific contract, a pre-bid meeting resolves queries, the contractor bids, the work is awarded to the lowest quote, a performance guarantee is deposited, and only then does site work begin.[^drhp-p72] Revenue therefore follows a pipeline of bids, not a recurring order stream. If the bids stop being accepted, the revenue stops.

The product list is concrete. On the railway side, the company designs and constructs platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion and track laying. On bridges, it performs contracts across beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed and truss bridges. On roads, it builds bituminous and concrete roads connecting highways and district roads within Maharashtra and across cities.[^drhp-p71] The second segment is the ready-mix concrete plant — a "state of the art" facility at Thane, in the company's own words.[^drhp-p63]

The client list is dominated by public bodies: Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation, Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority, Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India, plus a few private names like Sky Way Infrastructure, Dev Engineers, GCC Hotel and Krypton Corp.[^drhp-p63] Western Railway has been a client since 2006.[^drhp-p67]

Concentration is the defining business fact. Western Railway contributes 52% of the orders on hand, and the key customers as a group gave the company 57% of revenue in FY2018.[^drhp-p65][^drhp-p67] One client relationship, not a broad book, drives this company. Geography is narrow too: the DRHP calls the company "a major player in developing infrastructure in the city of Mumbai" that is "slowly and consolidating" its position in Maharashtra.[^drhp-p67] The company says its strength is experienced professionals with more than 20 years in the industry, and it uses the "box pushing" technique in executing projects.[^drhp-p67]

The company's own SWOT analysis lists the flip side: slow pace of government projects, working-capital crunch that may affect profitability, and changes in government policies.[^drhp-p76] These are not external observations; they are the company's own words about its weaknesses.

## The material risks, stated specifically

**Liquidity pressure.** The company had negative cash flow in recent financial years, carries heavy short-term borrowings, and had ₹405.02 lakh of unsecured loans repayable on demand as of March 31, 2018.
