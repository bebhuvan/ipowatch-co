# A B Infrabuild Limited: Inside the DRHP

A B Infrabuild is a Mumbai-based infrastructure contractor that builds railways, bridges, and roads, and operates a ready-mix concrete plant. It works almost entirely for government clients — Western Railway, Central Railway, the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai — and has been doing so for over a decade. [^drhp-p67] The company is now raising capital through an initial public offering of 44.28 lakh equity shares. The issue price has not yet been set. [^drhp-p29]

The numbers that matter before the details: promoter Amit Bholanath Mishra owns 90.949% of the company before the issue. [^drhp-p100] One customer — Indian Railways — contributed 57% of revenue in fiscal 2018. [^drhp-p12] Revenue has been declining for four straight years. [^drhp-p27] These are the facts a reader should hold in mind.

## What the company does

The company operates in two segments: infrastructure creation and ready-mix concrete (RMC) manufacturing. [^drhp-p63] The infrastructure side covers railway work — designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, and laying tracks — along with bridges (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss) and roads (bituminous and concrete). [^drhp-p71] The RMC plant is a state-of-the-art facility at Kashimira in Thane district, Maharashtra. [^drhp-p67]

Contracts are won through competitive bidding. The client floats a tender, a pre-bid meeting resolves queries, the contractor bids, and the lowest quote typically wins a Letter of Acceptance. The contractor then deposits a performance guarantee before work begins. [^drhp-p72]

The client list is dominated by government entities: Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation (MRVCL), Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA), and Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India. [^drhp-p63] Western Railway has been a client since 2006 [^drhp-p67] and contributed 52% of the order book. [^drhp-p65]

The company highlights expertise in box-pushing construction techniques, a strong order book, and is primarily active in Mumbai, consolidating its position across Maharashtra. [^drhp-p67]

The concentration risk is stark. Revenue from the top few customers accounted for 57% of total revenue in fiscal 2018. [^drhp-p67] That single number — more than half the company's income from a handful of government departments — is the most important fact about the business model.
