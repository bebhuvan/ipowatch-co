## What the company does

A B Infrabuild operates in two segments: infrastructure creation and ready-mix concrete (RMC) manufacturing. [^drhp-p63] The infrastructure side covers railway work — designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, and laying tracks — along with bridge construction (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss bridges) and road building (bituminous and concrete roads). [^drhp-p71] The RMC plant is located at Kashimira in Thane district, Maharashtra, and the company describes it as having state-of-the-art machinery and quality control systems. [^drhp-p67]

Contracts are won through a public tender process. The client floats a tender, a pre-bid meeting is held, contractors bid, and the job is awarded to the lowest quote. The winning contractor then deposits a performance guarantee. [^drhp-p72] This means the company's revenue depends entirely on winning bids in a competitive, price-sensitive process.

The company's major clients are all government or quasi-government entities: Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd (MRVCL), Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), and Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA). [^drhp-p63] This is not a diversified customer base. In Fiscal 2018, 57% of total revenue came from key customers, [^drhp-p67] and as of the DRHP date, Western Railway alone accounted for 52% of the orders on hand. [^drhp-p65] The company has worked with Western Railway since 2006. [^drhp-p67] Western Railway alone accounted for 52% of the orders on hand, so losing that single client would cut more than half the order book. [^drhp-p65]

The company describes itself as a major player in developing infrastructure in the city of Mumbai and says it is consolidating its position in Maharashtra with a strong order book. [^drhp-p67] It uses the "Box Pushing" technique in its projects, a method for constructing tunnels and underground structures without disrupting surface traffic. [^drhp-p67]
