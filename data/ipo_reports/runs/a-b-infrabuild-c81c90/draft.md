# A B Infrabuild Limited: Inside the DRHP

A B Infrabuild Limited is a civil contractor that builds railway platforms, bridges, and roads, and operates a ready-mix concrete plant in Thane. The company's revenue comes almost entirely from government and public-sector clients, with Indian Railways alone accounting for 57% of its FY2018 revenue [^drhp-p12]. It is now seeking to raise capital through an issue of 44,28,000 equity shares — the price has not yet been set [^drhp-p29] — with the proceeds earmarked primarily for working capital. The issue is managed by Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited [^drhp-p31].

## What the Company Does

A B Infrabuild earns revenue by bidding for and executing infrastructure contracts. The process begins when a client floats a tender, followed by a pre-bid meeting, bidding, and award to the lowest bidder. The contractor then deposits a performance guarantee and begins work. [^drhp-p72] The company undertakes projects across three broad categories: railways (designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, and track laying), bridges (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss), and roadways (bituminous and concrete roads). It also operates a ready-mix concrete plant at Kashimira in Thane district. [^drhp-p71] [^drhp-p67]

The company's client list reads like a who's who of Mumbai-area infrastructure agencies: Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd, Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority, Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, and private entities like Sky Way Infrastructure Private Limited. [^drhp-p63] But the dependence on a single client is stark. Western Railway alone contributes 52% of the orders on hand, and the top clients collectively accounted for 57% of revenue in FY2018. [^drhp-p65] [^drhp-p67]

The company cites several competitive strengths: long-standing client relationships (Western Railway has been a client since 2006) [^drhp-p67], modern techniques like box-pushing, state-of-the-art machinery, an experienced team with over 20 years in the industry, and a healthy order book. [^drhp-p67] It also acknowledges threats: the slow pace of government projects, working capital crunches, and changes in government policies. [^drhp-p76]
