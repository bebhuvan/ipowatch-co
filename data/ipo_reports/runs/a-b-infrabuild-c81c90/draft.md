# A B Infrabuild Limited: Infrastructure Contractor Sees Working Capital Injection in SME IPO

A B Infrabuild Limited is a Mumbai-based infrastructure contractor and ready-mix concrete manufacturer whose business depends almost entirely on winning government tenders — especially from Indian Railways. The company is bringing an SME IPO of 44,28,000 fresh equity shares, the proceeds of which will primarily go toward meeting working capital requirements. [^drhp-p29] [^drhp-p48]

The company operates in two distinct but related segments: infrastructure creation and operating a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant. [^drhp-p63] On the infrastructure side, it builds bridges (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss), constructs bituminous and concrete roads, and executes railway work including designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, and laying tracks. [^drhp-p71] Its RMC plant at Kashimira in Thane district supplies ready-mix concrete to construction sites. [^drhp-p63]

Contracts are won through a standard government tender process. A client floats a tender, the company bids, and the job is awarded to the lowest quote. Before work begins, the contractor must deposit a performance guarantee. [^drhp-p72] This means the company's revenue is not driven by marketing or sales in any conventional sense — it rises and falls with its success in competitive bidding.

## What the company does

The company's major clients read like a who's who of Mumbai and Maharashtra infrastructure authorities: Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd (MRVCL), Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA), and Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd. [^drhp-p63] Western Railway has been a client since 2006 and is by far the most important: it contributed 52% of the company's orders on hand and 57% of total revenue in fiscal 2018. [^drhp-p65] [^drhp-p67]

The company describes itself as a major player in developing infrastructure in Mumbai, slowly consolidating its position within Maharashtra. [^drhp-p67] It lists several competitive strengths: modern techniques such as the box-pushing method for executing projects, state-of-the-art machinery at its Thane RMC plant, and a team with more than 20 years of industry experience. [^drhp-p67]
