# A B Infrabuild Limited: An SME IPO with heavy railway reliance

A B Infrabuild Limited is an infrastructure construction company that builds railway platforms, station buildings, bridges, and roads, primarily in and around Mumbai.[^drhp-p71] It also operates a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant at Kashimira in Thane district.[^drhp-p63] The company is raising capital through a fresh issue of 44,28,000 equity shares of face value ₹10 each, at a price to be determined.[^drhp-p29] The proceeds are earmarked for working capital — ₹1,130 lakhs — and general corporate purposes, the amount of which has not been disclosed.[^drhp-p48]

This is an SME IPO, meaning the company is smaller than the threshold that would require a full main-board listing. The lead manager is Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited, and the registrar is Bigshare Services Private Limited.[^drhp-p31]

## What the company does

The company operates in two segments: infrastructure creation and operating a ready-mix concrete plant.[^drhp-p63] Its infrastructure work covers three broad categories. On railways, it designs and constructs platforms, station buildings, handles gauge conversion, and lays railway tracks. On bridges, it executes beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss bridge works. On roads, it builds bituminous and concrete roads connecting highways and district roads within the state and across cities.[^drhp-p71]

Contracts are won through a standard tender process. The client floats a tender, a pre-bid meeting is held for discussion and query resolution, the contractor bids, and the job is awarded to the lowest quote after the contractor deposits a performance guarantee.[^drhp-p72]

The company's marquee clients include the Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd, Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority, Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, and several private entities such as Sky Way Infrastructure Private Limited, Dev Engineers, GCC Hotel Private Ltd, and Krypton Corp.[^drhp-p63]

This client list reveals a critical concentration. Western Railway alone contributes 52% of the orders on hand and 57% of total revenue in Fiscal 2018.[^drhp-p65][^drhp-p67] The company's operations are concentrated in Mumbai and Maharashtra, and it describes itself as having a healthy order book.[^drhp-p67]

The company lists several competitive strengths in its filing: cost efficiency, timely delivery, technical expertise, quality, transparency in dealings with stakeholders, modern techniques such as box pushing, an experienced team with over 20 years in the industry, and state-of-the-art machinery at its RMC facility.[^drhp-p66][^drhp-p67]
