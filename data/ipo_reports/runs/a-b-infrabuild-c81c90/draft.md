# A B Infrabuild Limited: Inside the DRHP

A B Infrabuild Limited operates a ready-mix concrete plant in Thane, Maharashtra.[^drhp-p63] The company also builds railway stations, bridges, and roads.[^drhp-p71] The company wins contracts through government tenders, and its largest client — Western Railway — accounts for more than half of its order book.[^drhp-p72][^drhp-p65] Now it is raising money through an SME IPO, primarily to fund working capital.[^drhp-p29][^drhp-p48] The draft red herring prospectus (DRHP) reveals a business with falling revenue but a sharp profit spike in the most recent year, heavy short-term debt, and a handful of governance red flags that merit attention.

The company operates in two segments: infrastructure creation (railways, bridges, roadways)[^drhp-p71] and operating a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant at Thane.[^drhp-p63] Its project portfolio includes designing and constructing railway platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, laying tracks, building various types of bridges (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, truss), and constructing bituminous and concrete roads.[^drhp-p71]

Contracts are won through a standard tender process. The client floats a tender, a pre-bid meeting resolves queries, the contractor bids, and the job is awarded to the lowest quote. The winning contractor then deposits a performance guarantee before work begins.[^drhp-p72] This means revenue depends entirely on winning bids — there is no recurring income stream.

Major clients are predominantly government and public-sector entities: Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd, Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority, Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, and private firms such as Sky Way Infrastructure Private Limited, Dev Engineers, GCC Hotel Private Ltd, and Krypton Corp.[^drhp-p63]

## What the company does

The company's competitive strengths, as listed in the DRHP, centre on long-standing client relationships, technical capability, and management experience. Western Railway has been a client since 2006, and the company uses a box-pushing technique for executing projects — a method that allows construction without disrupting existing rail traffic.[^drhp-p67] The management team has more than 20 years of experience in the infrastructure industry.[^drhp-p67] The company has invested in state-of-the-art machinery and quality control systems at its RMC facility in Kashimira.[^drhp-p67] It describes itself as "a major player in developing infrastructure in the city of Mumbai" with a strong order book.[^drhp-p67]

The DRHP also emphasises quality and transparency in dealings with stakeholders, including clients, employees, and associates.[^drhp-p66]

But the single most important fact about the business is this: Western Railway contributes 52% of the orders on hand, and the company's top customers collectively account for 57% of total revenue for the year ended March 31, 2018.[^drhp-p65][^drhp-p67] That level of customer concentration is a structural risk — losing the railway account would cause a severe revenue drop.
