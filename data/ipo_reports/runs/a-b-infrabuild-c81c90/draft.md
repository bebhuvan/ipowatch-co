## What the company does

A B Infrabuild operates in two segments: creation of infrastructure and operation of a ready-mix concrete plant.[^drhp-p63] The infrastructure work spans railways, bridges and roadways — designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion and laying of railway tracks; performing contracts across various bridge types like beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed and truss bridges; and constructing bituminous and concrete roads connecting highways and district roads.[^drhp-p71] The concrete side operates a facility at Kashimira in Thane district, Maharashtra, with what the filing calls state-of-the-art machines and quality-control systems for ready-mix concrete (RMC) manufacturing.[^drhp-p67]

Revenue is won through a disclosed tender process: the client floats a tender for a specific work or contract; a pre-bid meeting resolves queries; the contractor bids; the job is awarded to the lowest quote, with a Letter of Acceptance issued to the lowest bidder; the contractor deposits a performance guarantee; and then the drawing is issued and the site handed over for execution.[^drhp-p72] The company's own strengths list is conventional — cost efficiency, timely delivery, technical expertise, quality, transparency —[^drhp-p66] but it does disclose a specific technique: "box pushing" for project execution, and leadership with more than 20 years of infrastructure industry experience.[^drhp-p67]

The client list is dominated by public-sector bodies.[^drhp-p63] Major customers named in the filing are Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd, Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority, Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, plus Sky Way Infrastructure Private Limited, Dev Engineers, GCC Hotel Private Ltd and Krypton Corp.[^drhp-p63] Western Railway has been a client since 2006[^drhp-p67] and contributes 52% of the orders on hand;[^drhp-p65] the top customers together contributed 57% of revenue for the year ended March 31, 2018.[^drhp-p67] The filing says the order book is strong,[^drhp-p67] but the only order-book figure in the extract is the Western Railway concentration — no absolute order-book value is disclosed in these pages.[^drhp-p65]
