## What the company does

The business splits into two segments: creating infrastructure, and operating a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant — that is, a facility that produces pre-mixed concrete delivered to construction sites. [^drhp-p63] The plant is at Kashimira in Thane district, Maharashtra. [^drhp-p67] The infrastructure side is specific about what it builds: railway platforms and station buildings, gauge conversion and track laying, bridges of several types — beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed and truss — and bituminous and concrete roads. [^drhp-p71] The RMC plant is the second segment. [^drhp-p63] The provided excerpts do not disclose a revenue split between the two segments.

How contracts are won matters more than what is built. The client floats a tender, the contractor bids, and the work is awarded to the lowest quote, which receives a Letter of Acceptance. The contractor then deposits a performance guarantee before the site is handed over. [^drhp-p72] This is a bid-driven, cost-competitive business. What the company sells is not a proprietary product but the ability to price low, deliver on time and execute technically — the filing's own list of strengths is cost efficiency, timely delivery, technical expertise and quality. [^drhp-p66]

The client list is dominated by public bodies: Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd, Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority and Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, plus private names like Sky Way Infrastructure, Dev Engineers, GCC Hotel and Krypton Corp. [^drhp-p63] The concentration is stark. Western Railway alone made up 52% of orders on hand, [^drhp-p65] and the top customers contributed 57% of total revenue in FY2018. [^drhp-p67] The company describes itself as a major player in developing infrastructure in Mumbai, slowly consolidating its position in Maharashtra with a strong order book, and notes that Western Railway has been a client since 2006.
