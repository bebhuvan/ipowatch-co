# A B Infrabuild Limited: Building on Railways, Betting on Growth

A B Infrabuild Limited is an infrastructure construction company that builds railway platforms, bridges, and roads, and operates a ready-mix concrete plant.[^drhp-p63][^drhp-p71] The company is raising capital through a fresh issue of shares, with the proceeds earmarked mainly for working capital.[^drhp-p29][^drhp-p48][^drhp-p67]

The offer comprises 44,28,000 equity shares of ₹10 each at a price that has not yet been set.[^drhp-p29] The lead manager is Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited, and the registrar is Bigshare Services Private Limited.[^drhp-p31]

## What the Company Does

A B Infrabuild operates in two segments: creation of infrastructure and operation of a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant.[^drhp-p63] The infrastructure work covers railways — designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, and laying tracks — as well as bridges of various types (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss) and roadways including bituminous and concrete roads.[^drhp-p71] The RMC facility is located at Kashimira in Thane district, Maharashtra.[^drhp-p67]

Contracts are won through a competitive tender process. The client floats a tender, a pre-bid meeting resolves queries, contractors bid based on eligibility, and the job is awarded to the lowest quote. The winning contractor then deposits a performance guarantee, receives the drawings, and takes site handover to begin execution.[^drhp-p72]

The company's major clients are all government or government-linked entities: Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation (MRVCL), Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA), and Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd., among others.[^drhp-p63] Western Railway has been a client since 2006 and contributed 52% of the orders on hand as of the date of the DRHP.[^drhp-p65][^drhp-p67] This concentration is extreme: the top customers together accounted for 57% of total revenue in fiscal 2018.[^drhp-p67]

Geographically, the company is focused on Mumbai and is slowly consolidating its position across Maharashtra.[^drhp-p67] It claims a healthy order book, though the total value is not disclosed in the excerpts.[^drhp-p67]

The DRHP highlights several advantages: use of modern techniques such as box-pushing for executing projects, an experienced management team with over 20 years in infrastructure, and an RMC facility.[^drhp-p67] The company also emphasises quality and cost efficiency in its dealings, and says it maintains transparency.[^drhp-p66]
