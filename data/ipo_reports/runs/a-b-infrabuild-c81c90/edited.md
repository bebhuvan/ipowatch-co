A B Infrabuild is an infrastructure contractor for government and public-sector clients in and around Mumbai, and it wants to raise money from the public.[^drhp-p63][^drhp-p13][^drhp-p29] The company runs two businesses: it builds railway platforms, station buildings, bridges and roads under contracts won through competitive tenders, and it operates a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant at Kashimira in Thane district, Maharashtra.[^drhp-p63][^drhp-p71][^drhp-p67] The draft red herring prospectus (DRHP) filed for the issue offers 44,28,000 equity shares of ₹10 each at a price that has not yet been set.[^drhp-p29] The single fact that frames everything else in the filing is this: Indian Railways contributed 57% of the company's revenue in the year ended 31 March 2018, and Western Railway alone accounts for 52% of the orders on hand.[^drhp-p12][^drhp-p65] A business this dependent on one customer is worth reading closely.

## What the company does

The company does not sell to an open market. It bids in tenders floated by clients, and the work goes to the lowest quote. The DRHP lays out the full six-step process: the client floats a tender for a specific work; a pre-bid meeting resolves queries; the contractor bids if eligible for that contract; the job is awarded to the lowest quote through a Letter of Acceptance; the contractor deposits a performance guarantee; and only then are drawings issued and the site handed over for execution.[^drhp-p72] This is a business model built on winning bids, not on building a brand.

The two segments are building infrastructure and operating a ready-mix concrete plant.[^drhp-p63] On the infrastructure side, the company designs and constructs railway platforms and station buildings; carries out gauge conversion and railway track laying; builds beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed and truss bridges; and constructs bituminous and concrete roads connecting highways and district roads within the state and across cities.[^drhp-p71] The RMC plant at Kashimira, Thane district, is where the company says it has invested heavily in machinery and quality-control systems to assure the quality of its services and its concrete.[^drhp-p67]

The client list is a roll-call of government and public-sector entities: Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, Western Railway, Central Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Ltd, Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority, Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd, plus private clients Sky Way Infrastructure Private Limited, Dev Engineers, GCC Hotel Private Ltd and Krypton Corp.[^drhp-p63] Western Railway has been a client since 2006.[^drhp-p67] The company describes itself as a major player in developing infrastructure in Mumbai, consolidating its position in Maharashtra with a strong order book.[^drhp-p67]

The concentration is the story. Western Railway, the major client, accounts for 52% of the orders on hand, and Indian Railways as a whole brought in 57% of total revenue in the year ended 31 March 2018.[^drhp-p65][^drhp-p67]
