# A G Universal Limited: A steel trader's SME IPO

A G Universal Limited is a trading company that buys and sells steel products, acting as a middleman between manufacturers and end-users. [^drhp-p95] The company has filed a Draft Red Herring Prospectus for an initial public offering of its equity shares on the SME platform of the National Stock Exchange. The offer size, the breakdown between fresh issue and offer for sale, and the price band are not disclosed in the available pages of the filing.

## What the company does

A G Universal does not manufacture steel. It trades it. The company sources products from manufacturers and sells them to customers, functioning as a dealer or distributor. Its product range includes ERW pipes (electric resistance welded pipes used in construction and infrastructure), MS pipes (mild steel pipes), hot and cold rolled strips and sheets, TMT bars, channels, angles, and beams — the basic building blocks of construction and engineering. It also trades in cPVC and uPVC pipes and fittings, which are used in plumbing and water supply systems. [^drhp-p95] [^drhp-p96] [^drhp-p97] [^drhp-p98]

The company acts as a dealer for several suppliers, including Surya Roshni Limited, Jindal Supreme (India) Private Limited, Swastik Pipe Limited, Ravindra Tubes Private Limited, and Sks Ispat & Power Ltd. [^drhp-p95] It does not manufacture any of the products it sells; its role is to buy from these suppliers and sell to its own customers, earning a margin on the difference.

All of A G Universal's revenue comes from within India. The filing reports no international sales in any of the periods shown. [^drhp-p100]

The company's competitive strengths, as listed in the DRHP, include the experience of its promoter and his network in the trading community, a quality policy, strengthened human capital, and existing client and supplier relationships. [^drhp-p98] [^drhp-p99] These are the standard claims a trading company makes: relationships matter more than technology, and the promoter's personal network is the business's primary asset.

## Key risks

The DRHP discloses several material risks that a prospective investor should consider.

**Customer concentration is extreme.** For the period ended November 30, 2022, the top 10 clients accounted for 92.61% of sales, and the top 5 contributed more than 75%. [^drhp-p26] The loss of any major customer would materially affect revenue and profitability. This is a structural risk for a trading company that depends on a handful of relationships.

**No long-term contracts with customers.** The company generally does business on a purchase order basis and does not enter into long-term contracts with most customers. [^drhp-p25] This means revenue is vulnerable to order cancellations or delays at any time. A customer who places a large order one quarter may place nothing the next.

**Price volatility in core products.** The prices of iron and steel products are subject to fluctuations due to domestic and foreign trade policies, shifts in supply and demand, and other factors beyond the company's control. [^drhp-p26] As a trader, A G Universal buys and sells at market prices; it cannot control the margin it earns if prices move against it between purchase and sale.

**Working capital dependency.** The company has not made alternate arrangements for working capital beyond its existing sanctioned limits. [^drhp-p27] A shortfall in working capital could disrupt operations. Trading businesses typically require significant working capital to finance inventory and receivables; any disruption in bank credit could be serious.

**Inadequate insurance.** The insurance coverage obtained by the company may not adequately protect against unforeseen losses. [^drhp-p28]

**No registered trademark.** The company does not own a registered trademark under the Trade Marks Act, 1999, and consequently does not enjoy the statutory protections accorded to a registered trademark. [^drhp-p25] This is unusual for a company that trades branded products and may limit its ability to protect its business name or brand.

**No independent monitoring of issue proceeds.** Because the issue size is less than ₹10,000 lakh, no independent monitoring agency will be appointed to oversee the deployment of the funds raised. [^drhp-p27] Investors must rely on the company's own reporting.

**Regulatory and political risks.** Changes in government regulations, taxes, or political instability could harm the business. [^drhp-p31] [^drhp-p32]
