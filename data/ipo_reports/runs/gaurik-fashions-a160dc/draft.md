# Gaurik Fashions Limited: inside the DRHP

Gaurik Fashions sells footwear under the Skechers brand through stores in northern India. [^drhp-p33] [^drhp-p34] The company has filed a Draft Red Herring Prospectus for an IPO that combines a fresh issue of up to 62,00,000 equity shares (face value ₹10 each, amount not disclosed) with an offer for sale of up to 8,00,000 equity shares by an investor selling shareholder. [^drhp-p100] The filing reveals a business that has grown rapidly — revenue more than tripled between FY23 and FY25 [^drhp-p235] — but carries specific risks around brand dependence, geographic concentration, and governance that a reader should weigh.

## What the company does

The majority of its revenue comes from footwear sales, and a significant portion of that revenue is concentrated in the northern region of India. [^drhp-p33] [^drhp-p34]

The company's most important brand relationship is with Skechers. A significant — though undisclosed — portion of revenue comes from Skechers stores. [^drhp-p33] This relationship is governed by non-exclusive agreements, meaning Skechers can appoint other retail partners or shift to selling directly to consumers. [^drhp-p38] The company also operates stores for Guess through its wholly owned subsidiary Gaurik Lifestyle Private Limited, and for Bugatti through Nuvora Retail Private Limited. [^drhp-p101] Part of the IPO proceeds will go into these subsidiaries to fund new stores and inventory. [^drhp-p101]

All of Gaurik's stores are on leased premises; the company does not own any real estate. [^drhp-p39] This creates renewal risk — if a landlord does not renew a lease, the company loses that store location and the revenue it generates.

## Key risks

The following risk factors are most specific to this company.

**Dependence on the Skechers brand.** A significant but undisclosed portion of revenue comes from Skechers stores. **Geographic concentration.** A significant portion of revenue comes from northern India. [^drhp-p34] Regional economic downturns, competitive pressures, or regulatory changes specific to that region would disproportionately affect the company's results.

**Lease renewal risk.** All stores are on leased premises. [^drhp-p39] If landlords do not renew leases or renegotiate on unfavourable terms, the company could lose store locations and the associated revenue. The filing notes that some lease agreements may not be registered under applicable law, which could affect their legal enforceability. [^drhp-p36]

**Auditor non-cooperation at a subsidiary.** The statutory auditor of Gaurik South Private Limited is not cooperating. [^drhp-p35] This is an unusual and serious disclosure.
