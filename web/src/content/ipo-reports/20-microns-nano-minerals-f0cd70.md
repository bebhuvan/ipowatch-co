20 Microns Nano Minerals Limited processes and sells specialty chemicals (functional additives, chemically modified minerals, soft minerals, and hard minerals) used across industries from paints to plastics to personal care. It is a small company listing on the SME platform of NSE (NSE EMERGE) to raise capital for a new manufacturing plant and to create a public market for its shares. The company relies on its corporate promoter, 20 Microns Limited, for marketing and sales through its network. [^drhp-p75-112]

The draft red herring prospectus (DRHP) filed with SEBI reveals a business that turned profitable from FY2015 after two years of losses, but whose operating cash flow remains volatile: negative in the most recent full financial year. [^drhp-p49-50] The offer price has not yet been set. The filing discloses no peer comparison data, making relative valuation impossible from the document alone.

## What the company does

20 Microns Nano Minerals sells four product categories: Functional Additives (FA), Chemically Modified Minerals (CMM), Soft Minerals (SM), and Hard Minerals (HM). These are specialty chemicals — substances formulated for specific performance rather than sold as commodities — and they find use in industries as varied as paints, plastics, rubber, adhesives, construction, and personal care. [^drhp-p112]

The company does not market its products independently. It has a long-term arrangement with its corporate promoter, 20 Microns Limited, to market and sell through that company's network. This is both a strength (the promoter's established distribution reach) and a dependency: any disruption in that relationship could severely impact revenue. [^drhp-p112-113]

<aside class="pull-quote">The company operates entirely on purchase orders, with no long-term or definitive agreements governing volume, pricing, or other terms of sale with customers.</aside>

The top 10 customers accounted for roughly 59%, 57%, and 59% of revenue from operations in FY2015, FY2016, and FY2017, respectively. [^drhp-p128] The company also exports, having shipped ₹1,068.18 lakh worth of products to Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, and Sri Lanka over the past five years. [^drhp-p128]

The DRHP lists six competitive strengths: a diversified product portfolio, diversified user industries, import substitution (replacing chemicals that India currently buys from abroad), the support of the holding company (20 Microns Limited), product innovation through continuous R&D, and a strong management team. [^drhp-p45-113]

The segment grew at 10–12% CAGR from fiscal 2014 to 2017, and CRISIL forecasts 12–14% CAGR going forward. Within specialty chemicals, certain sub-segments are expected to grow faster: construction chemicals and water chemicals at 15% CAGR each, personal care ingredients also at 15% CAGR, surfactants at 13% CAGR, and textile chemicals at 12% CAGR. [^drhp-p94]

The industry is highly fragmented, with small and medium enterprises (SMEs) holding 70–75% market share. Only a few Indian players have the scale or capability to compete with global giants on product development and innovation. [^drhp-p94-95]

Demand drivers include rising consumption, urbanisation (India's urban population was 31% in 2011), GDP growth (7.1% in fiscal 2017), and government initiatives such as the Draft National Chemical Policy, which aims to increase the chemicals sector's share of GDP. [^drhp-p87-95] Foreign direct investment of up to 100% is allowed in the sector. [^drhp-p96]

A favourable global factor: China, a major player in commodity chemicals, has seen stricter environmental regulations since 2015 that have impacted its chemical manufacturing output, potentially pushing buyers toward Indian suppliers. [^drhp-p96]

Regulatory context matters. The Goods and Services Tax (GST) came into effect in India from July 1, 2017, replacing multiple indirect taxes with a single tax. [^drhp-p111] Exports to the European Union must comply with the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) regulation. [^drhp-p110] The central government has approved four Petroleum, Chemicals and Petrochemicals Investment Regions (PCPIRs) (in Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Odisha) with total proposed investments of about ₹9 trillion. [^drhp-p111]

## The IPO offer

The DRHP states that the issue is being made to fund capital expenditure for setting up a new manufacturing facility at Plot 336, GIDC Waghodia, Vadodara, Gujarat, and for general corporate purposes. [^drhp-p75] A further objective is creating a public trading market for the company's equity shares by listing them on the SME Platform of NSE (NSE EMERGE), to enhance visibility and brand. [^drhp-p75]

The lead manager to the issue is Vivro Financial Services Private Limited. [^drhp-p54] The registrar is Link Intime India Private Limited. [^drhp-p55]

The DRHP does not disclose a split between fresh issue and offer for sale in the extracted facts. No selling shareholders are named. The offer price has not yet been set.

## Financial performance

The DRHP presents restated financial statements for five financial years (FY2013 through FY2017) and the six-month period ended September 30, 2017. All figures below are in ₹ lakh as reported in the filing.

### Profit after tax (PAT)

The company turned profitable from FY2015 onward. It reported losses of ₹27.40 lakh in FY2013 and ₹12.17 lakh in FY2014, then profits of ₹149.58 lakh in FY2015, ₹90.18 lakh in FY2016, and ₹174.82 lakh in FY2017. For the six months to September 2017, PAT was ₹132.75 lakh. [^drhp-p49]

### Earnings per share (EPS)

Basic EPS (in ₹) tracks the PAT trajectory: negative ₹0.45 in FY2013, negative ₹0.20 in FY2014, then positive ₹2.47 in FY2015, ₹1.46 in FY2016, ₹2.43 in FY2017, and ₹1.48 for the six months to September 2017. [^drhp-p177]

## Key risks from the filing

**No long-term customer or supplier agreements.** The company operates entirely on purchase orders, with no long-term or definitive agreements governing volume, pricing, or other terms of sale with customers. Similarly, it has no long-term agreements with raw material suppliers. This makes revenue and margins vulnerable to sudden changes in demand or input costs. [^drhp-p25-26]

**Negative operating cash flow in FY2017.** The company generated ₹(349.75) lakh from operations in FY2017, a dramatic reversal from ₹802.59 lakh in FY2016. Sustained negative cash flow could constrain growth and debt servicing. The filing itself flags this risk. [^drhp-p22-50]

**Missing statutory licenses for one manufacturing facility.** The company has not obtained certain statutory and regulatory licenses required to operate its facility at Plot 9–10, GIDC Waghodia, Vadodara, Gujarat. It cannot assure that it will not be penalised, which could disrupt operations. [^drhp-p19]

**Heavy reliance on corporate promoter for marketing and sales.** The company's long-term arrangement with 20 Microns Limited to market and sell its products through that company's network is a core dependency. Any disruption in that relationship could severely impact revenue. [^drhp-p112-113]

**Legal proceedings.** The company, its promoters, group companies, and directors are parties to certain legal proceedings. Any adverse decision could materially affect financial condition and results of operations. [^drhp-p17]
