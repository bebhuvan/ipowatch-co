A B Infrabuild Limited is an infrastructure construction company that also operates a ready-mix concrete (RMC) plant, focused on government and public-sector projects in and around Mumbai. The company works in two segments: building infrastructure — railways, bridges, roads — and manufacturing RMC at a facility in Kashimira, Thane.[^drhp-p63-71] It is issuing 44,28,000 new equity shares of ₹10 each at a price to be determined.[^drhp-p29] The main use of the proceeds is ₹1,130 lakhs (₹11.30 crore) for working capital, with the remainder for general corporate purposes.[^drhp-p48] The sole promoter, Amit Bholanath Mishra, controls 74,95,476 equity shares, or 90.949% of pre-issue capital.[^drhp-p100]

## What the company does

A B Infrabuild competes for contracts through a competitive tender process. The client floats a tender, a pre-bid meeting is held, and the job is awarded to the lowest bidder. The contractor then deposits a performance guarantee.[^drhp-p72] The company's project portfolio spans railways (designing and constructing platforms, station buildings, gauge conversion, and laying railway tracks), bridges (beam, cantilever, arch, suspension, cable-stayed, and truss), and roadways (bituminous and concrete roads connecting highways and district roads).[^drhp-p71]

Revenue is highly concentrated. Western Railway alone contributed 52% of the order book as of the latest period.[^drhp-p65] The company's top customers, including Western Railway, Central Railway, Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai, MMRDA, and others, accounted for 57% of total revenue in FY2018.[^drhp-p67] Western Railway has been a client since 2006.[^drhp-p67]

<aside class="pull-quote">Western Railway alone contributed 52% of the order book as of the latest period.</aside>

The second segment, ready-mix concrete (RMC), provides manufacturing revenue but its split is not disclosed separately in the filing.[^drhp-p63] The company cites quality, transparency, long-standing client relationships, use of box-pushing technique, experienced management, and a healthy order book as competitive strengths.[^drhp-p66-67]

## Industry and market

The infrastructure sector contributes roughly 8% to India's GDP and is highly fragmented with many unorganized players.[^drhp-p58] Demand drivers include government flagship programs such as 100 Smart Cities Mission, Atal Mission for Urban Rejuvenation and Transformation (AMRUT), Make in India, and Power for All, along with eased FDI norms in 15 sectors including real estate and construction development, and rising urbanization and disposable income.[^drhp-p58-59]

India has a requirement of investment worth ₹ 50 trillion (US$ 777.73 billion) in infrastructure by 2022; the logistics sector is growing at a CAGR of 10.5% from 2017 to 2020.[^drhp-p22-59] Competition comes from both organized and unorganized players; the market is working-capital-intensive and subject to slow government project execution and policy changes.[^drhp-p76]

## The IPO structure

The issue is of 44,28,000 equity shares of face value ₹10 each. The issue price will be determined later.[^drhp-p29]

Net proceeds after issue expenses are to be used for: (i) meeting working capital requirements of ₹1,130 lakhs; (ii) general corporate purposes (amount not disclosed).[^drhp-p48] The lead manager to the issue is Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited; the registrar is Bigshare Services Private Limited.[^drhp-p31] The company has not appointed an independent agency to appraise the project and will rely on internal monitoring by the audit committee.[^drhp-p18]

## Financial performance (₹ crore)

Revenue from operations fell from ₹90.21 crore in FY2015 to ₹60.53 crore in FY2018. The earlier peak was ₹90.21 crore (FY2015); FY2014 was ₹86.61 crore.[^drhp-p27]

Net profit after adjustments was volatile: ₹0.96 crore in FY2014, ₹0.78 crore in FY2015, ₹0.33 crore in FY2016, ₹0.16 crore in FY2017, and a sharp jump to ₹3.1466 crore in FY2018.[^drhp-p27] EBITDA (Profit before interest, depreciation and tax) ranged from ₹7.83 crore (FY2016) to ₹9.46 crore (FY2015). In FY2018 it was ₹9.29 crore.[^drhp-p111]

## Ownership and governance

Promoter Amit Bholanath Mishra holds 74,95,476 equity shares, constituting 90.949% of the pre-issue paid-up capital. Other directors hold only 300 shares each.[^drhp-p93-100] Key management personnel include CFO Anchal Pachori (age 28, CA) and Company Secretary Mohit Soni (age 26, ACS).[^drhp-p97]

Related-party transactions in FY2018: loans from KMP/board ₹68.4 lakh, director remuneration ₹57.7 lakh, and outstanding payables to related parties ₹324.3 lakh as on March 31, 2018.[^drhp-p129-130] Two companies promoted by Amit Bholanath Mishra, Al-Noor Chickens Private Limited and A B Magma Automobiles Private Limited, were struck off by the ROC in March 2016 due to non-operation.[^drhp-p18]

No independent monitoring agency has been appointed for fund utilisation; the company will rely on its audit committee.[^drhp-p18] There are no criminal cases filed against the directors, and they are not on the RBI list of willful defaulters.[^drhp-p90]

## Material risks from the filing

1. **Customer concentration**: Indian Railways (primarily Western Railway) contributed 57% of revenue in FY2018. Loss of this client would severely affect revenue and profitability.[^drhp-p12]

2. **Negative cash flows**: The company reported negative cash flows in recent financial years, which could impair its ability to fund operations and working capital needs.[^drhp-p16]

3. **Unsecured loans repayable on demand**: Unsecured loans of ₹405.02 lakh as on March 31, 2018 are repayable on demand. A sudden repayment call could strain liquidity.[^drhp-p16]

4. **Related-party transactions**: The company has entered into significant related-party transactions and may continue to do so, which could raise governance concerns.[^drhp-p15]

5. **Order book uncertainty**: Contracts in the order book may be cancelled, adjusted, or suspended by clients, meaning the order book is not a guaranteed indicator of future revenue.[^drhp-p14]
